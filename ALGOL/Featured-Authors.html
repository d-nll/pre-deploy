Inday Sara Duterte

Inday Sara Duterte, the Vice President of the Philippines, is not only a prominent political figure but also an influential author featured at Algorithm of Novels. She has captured the attention of readers nationwide with her groundbreaking book, Isang Kaibigan, which has become a literary phenomenon in the Philippines.

In Isang Kaibigan, Duterte explores the themes of friendship, loyalty, and resilience, drawing from her personal experiences and cultural values. The book delivers a powerful message about unity and the importance of fostering strong connections in a rapidly changing world. Her unique perspective, shaped by her role in governance and her deep connection to the Filipino people, brings a fresh and compelling voice to Philippine literature.

Duterte's work has resonated with readers from all walks of life, as it emphasizes the power of relationships and the strength found in community. Her ability to seamlessly blend real-world insights with heartwarming narratives makes Isang Kaibigan a must-read.

As a featured author at Algorithm of Novels, Inday Sara Duterte showcases her talent not only as a leader but also as a storyteller, leaving a lasting impact through the pages of her book.

Matt Haig

Matt Haig is an internationally acclaimed author, best known for his bestselling novel The Midnight Library. Through alternate lives, the book explores themes of regret, choices, and second chances, offering a profound reflection on life's possibilities.

In How to Stop Time, Haig tells the story of a man who ages slowly, spanning centuries, while grappling with timeless human emotions of love and loss. This novel, like much of Haig's work, beautifully examines time, memory, and human connection.

As a featured author at Algorithm of Novels, Matt Haig captivates readers with his compassionate storytelling and insightful explorations of mental health, identity, and resilience. His works offer comfort and thought-provoking perspectives that resonate deeply with a global audience.
