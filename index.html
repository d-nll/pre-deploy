About Me

Hi, I’m Denielle B. Ubana, a Bachelor of Science in Aviation Electronics Technology student at WCC Aeronautical & Technological College - North Manila. I specialize in web development, cinematography, video editing, multimedia. Combining creativity with technical skills to produce impactful work.

As a former Information Technology student, I have a solid foundation in both technology and creativity. I also served as a Senior High School Student Council Electoral Board member and contributed as the public materials artist at Dr. Carlos S. Lanting College.
